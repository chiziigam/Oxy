Rules

  • Radii follow each other: card 18, item 12 (card padding 6), button 12, small button 9, chip and message box fully round.
  • Spacing steps are 4, 8, 12, 16, 24. Screen edge padding is 16. Rows inside a card are 6 apart.
  • One level of nesting only: group card, then item rows. Controls inside a row have no box of their own.
  • Frosted only on the header and message box when a conversation scrolls under them. Cards are plain.
  • One accent for buttons and the selected chip. Warning and danger are used for state only.
  • Font is Geist everywhere. Times use tabular figures.

Motion and haptics

Haptics are kept for results that matter and for controls, because Apple advises against overusing them. Animations follow the phone's Reduce Motion setting. Both can be tried on the earlier appearance page.

Dropped, and what stays

These parts of the old app are not in this design.

  • Memory page. It wasn't part of what the app is for.
  • Full history page. The Done list covers it.
  • Paired displays. Not needed with a speaker and a phone app.
  • Import from ChatGPT or Claude.
  • Private mode. It only opened Settings.
  • Four colour themes and the opening-style option. Replaced by theme and accent.
  • The round logo menu, the wheel and any app name in the interface.

The two plugs have no screen. They appear in Home as devices if they report anything.

Sources

  • Apple, Materials: frosted glass on the control layer only, never glass on glass.
  • Use Your Loaf, SwiftUI Sensory Feedback: built-in haptic styles.
  • Pow: ping and shake effects for the real app.
  • Nielsen Norman Group, smart device best practices: clear status, visible progress, feedback that explains failures, devices named by room.
